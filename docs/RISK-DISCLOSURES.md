# Risk & Disclosures — Phase I

DataTrust materials distinguish project vision, current product capabilities, planned protocol architecture, token functionality and presale participation.

Nothing in the Phase I interface should be read as a promise of return, guaranteed appreciation, guaranteed liquidity, regulatory approval, exchange listing, partnership, audit, revenue, user traction, transaction volume or operational capability unless supported by a canonical source.

A technically functioning presale does not establish authorization to accept real participant funds. Real-money activation requires separate legal, regulatory, operational, custody/payment and compliance readiness review.

## Claims requiring legal review

- characterization of $DTR under applicable law
- eligibility and jurisdiction restrictions
- marketing language around participation
- accepted payment assets and custody/payment routing
- refund/cancellation terms
- allocation and vesting disclosures
- privacy/data protection notices
- tax treatment

## Source rule

When investor materials conflict, the UI must not silently choose a value. Mark the item `CONFLICTING SOURCE — REQUIRES DECISION` and isolate it behind canonical configuration/content.
